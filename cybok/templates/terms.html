Terms & Privacy Notice

Plain-language summary for this Cybok installation.

Your data

  • Your conversations, files, images, logos and settings are private to your account. Other users cannot see them.
  • When you send a message, its text (and the text of attached files) is sent to the AI provider of the model you selected (for example Groq, OpenRouter, Pollinations.ai, or a local Ollama server) to generate the answer. Each provider has its own terms.
  • You can export all your data or permanently delete your account from Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Shared links only show the shared conversation — never your name, e-mail or other chats — and you can disable them at any time.

Acceptable use

  • Don't use Cybok to create illegal, harmful or abusive content, or to violate others' rights.
  • AI can make mistakes. Check important information before relying on it.
  • Generated images and text are provided as-is. Respect the terms of the underlying model providers.

AI models

Cybok uses openly available models (such as Meta Llama, gpt-oss and FLUX) through their providers' official APIs or self-hosted servers. The model in use is always shown in the chat.
