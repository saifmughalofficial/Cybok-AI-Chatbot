Questions

Frequently asked

Is Cybok really free?

Yes. Cybok uses free, openly licensed AI models. The default model and image generator work without any API key. Optional extra models use free accounts from Groq or OpenRouter, or run on your own computer with Ollama.

Is my data private?

Your chats, files and creations are private to your account. Messages are sent to the selected model's provider only to generate answers. You can export or delete everything at any time.

What files can I analyze?

PDF, DOCX, PPTX, ODT, TXT, Markdown, CSV, XLSX, JSON, HTML, most code files and images (PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF).

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — Cybok is fully responsive and can be installed to your home screen like an app.
